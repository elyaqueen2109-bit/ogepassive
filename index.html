Passive Voice: Тренажер для ОГЭ

📖 Правило: Времена Passive Voice

Формула пассивного залога: be + V3 (Past Participle)

Время Формула Пример (Active → Passive)
Present Simple am / is / are + V3 They clean the room. → The room is cleaned.
Past Simple was / were + V3 She broke the window. → The window was broken.
Future Simple will be + V3 They will send the letter. → The letter will be sent.
Present Perfect have / has been + V3 He has done the task. → The task has been done.
Past Perfect had been + V3 She had finished the work. → The work had been finished.

Упражнение 1. Раскройте скобки, употребляя глаголы в Passive Voice. Определите время по контексту.

1. This bridge (build) in 1995.
2. The room (clean) every day.
3. The letters (send) tomorrow.
4. English (speak) all over the world.
5. The dinner (already / cook).
6. The homework (do) every day.
7. These houses (not / sell) yet.
8. The window (break) by the boys yesterday.

Упражнение 2. Преобразуйте предложения из Active в Passive.

1. They make cars in Japan. → Cars in Japan.
2. Someone stole my bike last week. → My bike last week.
3. They will publish the book next month. → The book next month.
4. People have used this computer for many years. → This computer for many years.
5. They have already finished the task. → The task already.
